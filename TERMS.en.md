# Terms of Service

> Last updated: 2026-04-16
> Status: Operational draft. This document should be finalized through legal review before the paid service fully launches.

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the terms for using NomadNavigator AI (NNAI), including paid subscription operation, cancellation, and refund standards.

This is an operational draft intended to clarify service principles. The final version may be adjusted according to applicable law, payment provider policies, and consumer protection rules. If applicable law conflicts with this document, the law takes precedence.

## 2. Nature of the Service

NNAI is an informational AI service for digital nomads and people preparing for immigration or long-term relocation.

- City recommendations
- Visa, budget, and tax-related guidance
- Comparison and planning support

Information provided by NNAI is for reference only and does not constitute legal advice, tax advice, immigration agency services, or investment advice. Users are responsible for final decisions and should consult local professionals where needed.

## 3. Accounts and Payments

- Login is based on Google OAuth.
- Paid features may be provided through a subscription or usage-based billing model.
- Payments may be processed through Polar or another external payment platform selected by the company.
- Users must provide valid payment methods and accurate account information at checkout.

## 4. Paid Plans and Entitlements

- Payment completion and in-app access may not be reflected at exactly the same time.
- Actual service access is determined by NNAI's internal entitlement status.
- A short delay may occur after payment while webhooks or payment status synchronization complete.
- Normal paying customers should not be immediately blocked due only to a short synchronization delay.

## 5. Subscription Renewal and Cancellation

- Monthly subscriptions may renew automatically unless stated otherwise.
- If a user cancels a subscription, billing stops from the next billing cycle.
- The current paid period may remain available until the end of that period.
- Cancellation requests may be handled through the payment platform customer portal or NNAI support.

## 6. Refund Principles

NNAI aims to support paid customers in good faith. However, because this is a digital service, not every payment is automatically refundable.

### 6-1. Full Refund Review

NNAI generally reviews full refunds in the following cases:

- Duplicate payment or duplicate charge
- Payment completed, but paid features were not usable due to a technical error and were not restored within a reasonable period
- Clearly incorrect charge or system malfunction
- Confirmed unauthorized payment

### 6-2. First-Time Paid Customer Protection

If a first-time paid subscriber finds the service materially different from expectations, NNAI may review a refund under the following conditions:

- Request is made within 7 days of the first payment date
- There was no abnormally excessive usage
- PAYG or additional usage-based charges have not already occurred at large scale

The company prioritizes customer experience and will generally review eligible cases in a customer-friendly manner.

### 6-3. Renewal Payment Refunds

Recurring renewal payments are generally not automatically refundable for the full period once the period has started. Exceptions may be reviewed for full or partial refunds in cases such as:

- The request was made very shortly after renewal and there was almost no actual usage
- A system outage prevented core features from being provided during the renewal period
- Support recognizes special circumstances

### 6-4. PAYG Refunds

Usage-based billing may be tied to actual processing costs, so normally delivered usage is generally not refundable. Refunds or adjustments may be reviewed for:

- Duplicate charges
- Incorrect usage records caused by a system bug
- Unauthorized use
- Company-side outage where the feature failed but billing still occurred

### 6-5. Review Factors

The company may consider the following factors:

- Payment date and request date
- Actual feature usage
- Whether an outage or error occurred
- Payment platform records
- Account security issues or signs of unauthorized use

## 7. How to Request a Refund

Refund requests can be sent to:

- `nnai.support@gmail.com`

Helpful information to include:

- Account email
- Payment date and time
- Payment amount
- Description of the issue
- Whether the case involves duplicate payment, malfunction, or non-use

The company will review requests within a reasonable period and may request additional information if needed.

## 8. Payment Synchronization Delays and Customer Protection

The following may occur shortly after payment:

- Delay in entitlement reflection after checkout
- Webhook delay or failure
- Temporary payment platform issue

In these cases, the company will respond where possible in this order:

1. Automatic synchronization retry
2. `restore purchase` or status recheck
3. Grace status if needed
4. Manual recovery

Normal paying customers should not be disadvantaged for a long period due only to synchronization delay.

## 9. Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct may result in service restriction or refund denial:

- Excessive automated calls through scraping, bots, or similar tools
- Account sharing or unauthorized resale
- Payment abuse, chargeback abuse, or repeated refund abuse
- Abnormal use that harms service stability

## 10. Limitation of Liability

The company works to improve service stability and information accuracy, but does not guarantee:

- Immigration approval outcomes
- Visa issuance outcomes
- Tax outcomes
- Final success of an investment or residence decision

Users remain responsible for verifying legal, tax, and administrative consequences before making decisions.

The company may update this document due to service changes, legal changes, or payment structure changes. Important changes will be announced through reasonable methods.
