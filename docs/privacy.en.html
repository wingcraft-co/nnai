Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

NomadNavigator AI ("service", nnai.app) values user privacy and complies with applicable privacy laws.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google login: name, email address, and profile photo provided by Google OAuth
  • Service usage information: recommendation inputs such as nationality, income, and lifestyle preferences
  • Automatically collected information: IP address, browser type, and access time through server logs
  • Product analytics information: within the scope of consent, usage events such as page path, language, CTA clicks, recommendation submission/success/failure, login/payment flows, and result card interactions (PostHog)

2. Purpose of Collection

  • Providing AI city recommendations
  • Providing saved city and pin features
  • Managing paid custom report purchases and access
  • Improving service quality and analyzing errors

3. Third-Party Disclosure

We generally do not provide collected personal information to third parties. However, when Google AdSense ads are displayed, Google's privacy policy may apply.

4. Analytics Tools and Consent Settings

The service uses PostHog Cloud EU to analyze service flow and conversion funnels. Analytics operate in two levels depending on the user's choice.

  • Essential analytics only: only minimal anonymous cookieless tracking is enabled. Persistent cookie-based identification, session replay, and autocapture are not used.
  • Allow all: extended analytics may be enabled, including cookie-based persistent tracking, autocapture, and session replay. Full consent features may be rolled out gradually depending on the deployment environment.

No PostHog event collection starts before the user makes a choice.

In Essential analytics only mode, the service may collect the following events.

  • Page views, landing CTA clicks, recommendation submission/success/failure
  • Login entry/click/success, pricing page visits, checkout clicks/returns
  • Exact income or budget values, free-text inputs, email, name, and OAuth identifiers are not sent to PostHog.

In Allow all mode, additional product analytics may be included, such as autocapture, session replay, onboarding dwell time, form abandon events, result card interactions, and pricing section engagement.

Analytics settings can be reopened and changed at any time from the footer's Cookie button or the Privacy Policy page.

5. Advertising and Cookies

The service uses Google AdSense and may use cookies for ad delivery. Google's ad privacy settings can be managed at adssettings.google.com.

6. Retention and Deletion

Information is deleted immediately upon account deletion request or service termination. Server logs are retained for up to 30 days and then deleted automatically.

7. User Rights

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, contact us by email.
